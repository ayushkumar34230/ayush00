Creative Journey

About the Artist

The story, philosophy, and artistic exploration behind My Art Gallery.

Elena Rostova

Principal Artist & Studio Founder

Born with an innate passion for texture, color harmony, and natural light, Elena has spent over 15 years developing a distinctive style that bridges classical European impressionism with dynamic contemporary abstraction.

Working predominantly in high-grade oils and cold-pressed watercolor pigments, each artwork is created through intentional layers—sometimes requiring months of slow glazes and contemplative palette-knife sculpting.

"My mission is to create art that transforms living spaces into spaces of contemplation, beauty, and emotional peace."
